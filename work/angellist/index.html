AngelList I joined AngelList in 2022 as a Principal Product Designer to design tools for venture capitalists and startup founders. When I started, I knew very little about how VCs actually worked: their workflows, the tools they used, or the problems they faced day to day. I spent a lot of time talking to LPs and VCs, understanding their world, and learning about the fragmented tools they were using. During my time there, I designed multiple zero-to-one products that helped them manage their investments in ways that felt natural to how they actually worked.
