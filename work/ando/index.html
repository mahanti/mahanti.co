Ando Ando was a delivery-only restaurant by Momofuku, in partnership with Expa. When I joined, it was still very early. We were figuring out what Ando would become. During my time there, I was responsible for designing multiple zero-to-one products, from the app customers used to place orders to the menu management system chefs used to track inventory. I also led our food photography and packaging efforts, learning a lot about an industry I knew very little about when I started.

Ando was acquired by Uber in 2017, which was one of those moments you'll remember for a long time: seeing something you helped build become part of something much larger.

The Ando customer app interface for ordering food delivery. This was one of those projects where the complexity is deceptively high. Behind what looks like a simple ordering flow lies a lot of work understanding how people think about ordering food, what information they need, and what actions they need to take quickly.

I worked with Christina Tosi and the Milkbar team to design packaging for our co-branded Milkbar cookies. This was one of those projects where collaboration just happened and people felt empowered to create. Working with Christina and her team taught me a lot about how to think about packaging: not just as a container, but as part of the experience.

I was the food photographer for Ando, taking photos for both menu items and marketing materials. I had never done food photography before, so I learned a lot along the way: figuring out lighting, composition, and how to make food look as good in a photo as it does in person. This was one of those projects where I got to wear many hats and learn skills I never expected to need.
