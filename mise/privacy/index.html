Mise Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mise ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the Mise iOS application.

What We Collect

Sign in with Apple

When you sign in with Apple, Apple provides Mise with a unique identifier and, if you choose to share it, your name. We use this identifier solely to associate your subscription and your recipe library with your account. We do not receive your email address unless you choose to share it. We do not sell or share this information with any third party.

Your Recipes and Library

Recipes, books, menus, notes, and photos you save are stored on your device and synced across your devices via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). This data lives in your personal iCloud account — we do not have access to it, and we cannot read its contents.

AI Features

When you use AI-powered features in Mise (recipe scanning, link parsing, or Chef chat), the relevant text or image is sent to Anthropic's API via our infrastructure for processing. We do not log or retain the content of these requests beyond what is required to deliver the response. We do not use your content to train any AI model.

Anonymous Usage Telemetry

Mise records anonymous events about how the app is used — for example, which features are opened and whether an action succeeded or failed. These events are not linked to your identity, your Apple ID, or your recipe content. We use them to understand which features work well and which need improvement.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We receive a signed receipt from Apple confirming your subscription status. We do not receive your payment information or any details about your Apple ID payment method.

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell your data, ever.
  • We do not have access to your recipes or photos.

Data Sharing

We share data only with the following service providers, each strictly limited to the purpose described:

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple, iCloud sync, and subscription processing.
  • Anthropic — for processing AI requests when you use AI features.
  • Cloudflare — to deliver telemetry and AI requests reliably.

Household Sharing

If you choose to share your recipe library with a household member via Mise's sharing feature, that person receives access to the recipes you share through Apple's CloudKit sharing. We do not see or store this share relationship.

Children

Mise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by signing out and removing the app, which clears local data, or by deleting your iCloud data via the Apple iCloud settings. For any questions about your data, email us at the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy as Mise evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
