Common questions

How do I save a recipe from Instagram or TikTok?

Take a screenshot, then open Mise and import from your Photos. Mise reads the screenshot and saves the recipe automatically. You can also use the iOS share sheet on a recipe link from Safari or any browser.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions and choose Mise. You can also cancel from inside Mise via Account → Subscription.

How does the free trial work?

New Premium subscribers get a 7-day free trial. You can cancel any time before the trial ends and won't be charged. If you don't cancel, the trial converts to a paid subscription at the price shown when you signed up.

I deleted the app — are my recipes gone?

No. Your recipes sync to your iCloud account, so they come back when you reinstall and sign in with the same Apple ID.

Does Mise work offline?

Your saved recipes are available offline. AI features (recipe scanning, Chef chat) need an internet connection.

What if a scanned recipe is wrong?

AI parsing isn't perfect. You can edit any recipe — tap the recipe, then tap Edit. If something is consistently broken, please email us so we can improve it.
