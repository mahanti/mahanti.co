zoon privacy policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

zoon is an independent Apple Music player for iPhone, Mac, and Apple TV, developed by Mango Studios / Arjun Mahanti.

Apple Music

With your permission, zoon uses Apple's MusicKit APIs to access your music library, playlists, favorites, and Apple Music catalog resources so it can display and play music and perform the library actions you request. Subscription catalog streaming requires an active Apple Music subscription. Apple operates Apple Music and processes its account and service data under Apple's privacy policy.

Device storage and iCloud

zoon stores preferences, recent searches, cached library information, and playback state locally on your device. Pinned music identifiers and display metadata are also saved in your private iCloud key-value storage to synchronize pins between your devices using the same iCloud account. Apple operates iCloud under Apple's privacy policy. Pin synchronization depends on iCloud availability and your account settings.

Information available to the developer

zoon does not send your listening history, music library, searches, or personal information to developer-operated servers. zoon does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. Your Apple Music authorization does not give the developer access to your Apple Account password.

Your choices

You can manage Apple Music access and iCloud account settings on your device. You can remove pins within zoon; removals synchronize when iCloud is available. Removing the app removes app-local data according to the platform's storage behavior and does not by itself guarantee removal of previously synchronized iCloud data.

Changes and contact

Any material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
