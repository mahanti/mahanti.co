Terraforms On-chain land
About Terraforms is an extremely complex, beautiful on-chain art project. This small utility was part learning exercise for SwiftUI and part convenience tool for shuffling through all the parcels. When I started building this, I knew very little about on-chain art or how Terraforms worked. I learned a lot along the way, figuring out how to make something that felt natural for exploring something so complex. The majority of the product is a wrapper for the fantastic Terraform Explorer by @el_ranye, which was one of those moments where collaboration just happened and people felt empowered to create.
