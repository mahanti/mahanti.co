Circuit A simple, extremely efficient HIIT timer.

Get the app
About I often run through the same timers in the gym, whether for HIIT cycles or timing planks and stretches. I wanted to build an app with a timer that was easy to see from anywhere and could easily be used between sets. This was one of those projects that started from a personal need: I was frustrated with existing timer apps and wanted something simpler and more focused.

I worked with my friends at Shag City to develop custom sounds that add utility and delight to the app, even when you're exhausted from the last set. This collaboration was one of those moments you'll remember for a long time: working with friends on something that started as a simple idea and grew into something much more polished.

Save your favorite circuits and start them with a single tap. This was one of those features where the challenge was making something feel fast and effortless. You want to start your workout quickly, not spend time setting things up.

Quick defaults make it easy to jump into your favorite circuit the moment you open the app. This was one of those features where the mechanics and ergonomics mattered more than any specific interaction. The challenge was making something that felt natural whether you were starting your first circuit or your tenth.

The Circuit logo is a dynamic element that reflects your current circuit's work, rest, and cooldown periods. This was one of those features where working directly in code allowed me to explore ideas I might not have tried otherwise. The immediacy of seeing an animation work in real time changed how I thought about the design.

A simple calendar displays your circuit history and keeps you motivated. This was one of those features where the challenge was making something feel useful without being overwhelming. You want to see your progress, but you don't want it to feel like a chore.

Circuit comes with custom sounds designed to keep you on track. I worked with my friends at Shag City to create sounds that match the bouncy, friendly vibe of the app. This collaboration was one of those moments where working with friends on something creative opened up possibilities I might not have considered otherwise.

An app clip made it easy for customers to share and try Circuit without downloading the app. This was one of those features where the challenge was making something feel seamless and natural. You want people to be able to try the app quickly, without friction.
