Sudo I built Sudo over the course of a week to deliver a simple, straightforward, and delightful way to play Sudoku. This was one of those projects that started from a simple idea: I wanted to build something quickly, without overthinking it. In addition to classic Sudoku, Sudo lets you play in color, which was one of those features that emerged naturally as I worked on the app. Once I started exploring color, I found myself reaching for it everywhere.
