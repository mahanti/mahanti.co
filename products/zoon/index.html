--- layout: default title: zoon ---
zoon zoon is a simple wrapper for MusicKit that takes inspiration from classic iPod navigation and pairs it with modern iOS visuals and interactions. This was one of those projects where I found myself thinking about how we used to interact with music: the click wheel, the simplicity, the focus on the music itself rather than everything around it. I wanted to capture some of that feeling in a modern app.

zoon uses simple lists and gestures to navigate your music library. This was one of those features where the mechanics and ergonomics mattered more than any specific interaction. The challenge was making something that felt natural whether you were browsing your library or jumping to a specific album.

The player experience uses Metal shaders to create a subtle particle effect during playback. This was one of those features where working directly in code allowed me to explore ideas I might not have tried otherwise. The immediacy of seeing an effect work in real time changed how I thought about the design.
