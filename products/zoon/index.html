--- layout: default title: zoon ---
zoon zoon is a beautiful, gestural music player that blends the simplicity of classic iPod navigation with contemporary iOS visuals and interaction patterns. Under the hood, it’s a lightweight MusicKit wrapper that works seamlessly with your existing Apple Music library

zoon has two main surfaces. The browsing experience pairs a simple list with a customizable coverflow, making it easy to pin your favorite songs, albums, and playlists. The playback surface uses album art and dynamic particle effects to create a subtle and engaging experience while giving you access to playback controls.

Coverflow allows you to quickly navigate your library and pin your favorite songs, albums, and playlists. This makes it easy jump back into your favorite songs or albums with a single tap and update pins as you discover new music.

Most music streaming apps feel disjointed to me. They have mixed navigation and layout patterns, layouts that mix lists and grids, and views that combine music and podcasts in a single view. zoon relies on a simple list-based navigation structure and gestural interactions that enable consistent traversal of your libary without feeling heavy and clunky.

The player experience uses Metal shaders to create a subtle particle effect during playback.
